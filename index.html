Hi, I’m Briana.

I’m an artist and designer focused on drawing, painting, and digital work. My practice explores storytelling through visual composition, texture, and color.

Briana Cochrane is pursuing a Bachelor of Art in Graphic Design at Columbia College, building upon her Associates of Fine Arts from Moberly Area Community College. With experience in painting, drawing, and ceramics, Briana maintains a strong connection to traditional art practices. Although she is getting a design degree, she leans heavily towards physical art mediums as she has spent more time developing those skills. Even when creating digitally, Briana adds handmade elements to preserve a sense of craftsmanship.
