Hello, I'm Briana!

When creating artwork, I blur the line between digital and traditional media. Most of my experience with art has been rooted in traditional means, such as drawing, painting, and ceramics. It has only been within the past two years that I began to create digitally. I find it easier to connect to a piece I have created physically as it is tangible. I look for ways to make that connection with digital works. I create digitally as I plan to work in marketing and to bring designs to the masses. I aim to bring digital and traditional art together to create a sense of balance. Viewers should also be able to find that connection when engaging with digitally created pieces. By combining old techniques with new technology, my work serves as a reminder of our origins in an increasingly digital world.

Briana Cochrane is pursuing a Bachelor of Art in Graphic Design at Columbia College, building upon her Associates of Fine Arts from Moberly Area Community College. Briana is currently employed at DecorSign Inc where she is creating their brand bible and is establishing the visual tone for the company. With experience in painting, drawing, and ceramics, Briana maintains a strong connection to traditional art practices. Although she is getting a design degree, she leans heavily towards physical art mediums as she has spent more time developing those skills. Even when creating digitally, Briana adds handmade elements to preserve a sense of craftsmanship.
